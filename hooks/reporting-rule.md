## Reporting finished work

When you create or update a file, artifact, plan or doc, or carry out a plan:
- Don't restate what's in it. The user can open it.
- Reply in 1–3 lines: what was made or changed, its path or link, and only what needs the user's attention (failures, skipped steps, decisions you made for them, open questions).
- Give a summary or details only when the user asks.

This overrides any skill or instruction that says to summarize finished work.
